# Trust gates

- Ambiguity: require an observed collision or inconsistent identifiers, plus missing/disagreeing disambiguators on the audited site.
- Inconsistency: require two citable representations of the same material fact that cannot both be current; distinguish locale/currency/variant differences.
- Staleness: require an explicit date/status contradicted by a later authoritative source, an expired promise still framed as current, or a systematic lack of update metadata on content where time materially changes truth. Old publication date alone is not stale.
- Corroboration: weak coverage becomes a finding only when a material self-asserted claim lacks independent support after documented targeted searches; severity is medium or low unless false/conflicting evidence is verified.
- Entity markup: recommend `Organization`/appropriate subtype with stable URL, logo, contact, and truthful `sameAs` only when it resolves observed ambiguity or materially consolidates identity.
