# Trust gates

- Ambiguity: require an observed collision or inconsistent identifiers, plus missing/disagreeing disambiguators on the audited site.
- Inconsistency: require two citable representations of the same material fact that cannot both be current; distinguish locale/currency/variant differences.
- Staleness: require an explicit date/status contradicted by a later authoritative source, or an expired promise still framed as current. Old publication dates, copyright years, HTTP Last-Modified and missing update metadata alone do not establish stale facts.
- Corroboration: no independent search result is not a defect. Require a material contradictory source, unverifiable credential presented as independently certified, or an observed identity collision with insufficient disambiguation. A relevant evidence-backed opportunity may propose substantiating a decision-critical comparative claim; don't prescribe backlinks or manufactured mentions.
- Entity markup: recommend `Organization`/appropriate subtype with stable URL, logo, contact, and truthful `sameAs` only when it resolves observed ambiguity or materially consolidates identity.
