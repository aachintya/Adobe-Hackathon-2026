# Engagement gates

- Orientation: flag when title/H1/opening copy do not identify entity, offering, audience, or page purpose on a major landing template.
- Information scent: flag vague links/CTAs only when the destination/task cannot be predicted in context; quote labels and destinations.
- Continuity: from acquisition-like pages, test whether a user can reach detail, proof, comparison/pricing where applicable, and contact/next step without losing context. Record path and dead end.
- Context retention: check whether selected product/location/locale or prior step is visibly carried into the next public step. Do not expect personalization without an initiated context.
- Accessibility friction: missing form labels, keyboard traps, unreadable overlays, or image-only actions are findings only when directly observed and task-relevant.
- Trust/orientation proof: claims without nearby substantiation may be a medium/low issue; stylistic preferences are not findings.
- Performance: report only measured metrics with device/profile/sample; otherwise mark not checked.
